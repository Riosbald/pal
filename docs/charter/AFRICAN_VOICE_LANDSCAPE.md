# African Voice AI Landscape & M\u00cdMO Niche Lock

## Niche

Others build systems that **hear and speak** African languages (Maraba, Intron Sahara, N-ATLAS, NaijaVox).  
M\u00cdMO builds the layer that knows when **understanding is incomplete** and lets the speaker teach \u2014 before action.

STT providers are swappable. Activation is the moat.
