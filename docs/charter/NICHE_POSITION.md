# NICHE POSITION (not a lock)

**Status:** Positioning freeze · **Evidence:** open (2026-09-29)

## Direct answer

The conversation **defines** a defensible thesis. It does **not** lock a moat.

| Criterion | Established? |
|-----------|----------------|
| (a) Problem is real | Yes — cultural / discourse meaning loss under fluent ASR+LLM |
| (b) Needed commercially | **No** — unvalidated demand |
| (c) Layer thin / under-productized | Partially — HEAR/SPEAK crowded; UNDERSTAND thin, **not empty** |
| (d) Our system can hold it | **No** — demo + seed only; calibration unproven |

**Defined, yes. Locked, no.**

## Defensible position (use this wording)

> **MÍMO detects when representation may be inadequate and gates action until understanding is sufficient or corrected by the speaker.**

Not: “MÍMO knows when understanding is incomplete.”  
Coverage and completeness are **inferences**. We detect *possible* inadequacy; we do not prove completeness.

## Workable lessons from Sakana (adapted)

| Workable | Not workable |
|----------|----------------|
| Specialize a strong open base with curated in-domain data — **one language/community at a time** | Train “Africa” as one monoculture |
| Domain-specific evaluation is the moat (Anti-Coconut / discourse-native suite) | Generic BLEU/WER as cultural adequacy |
| Failures as high-information cases → regression tests first | Weight updates before evaluation discipline |
| Recursive learning = failures→tests→human gate | Autonomous ontology rewrite |
| Orchestration of models inside the semantic core | Multi-model mesh as the product story |
| Sovereignty: data, eval, provenance, deploy control | Sovereignty as marketing footnote |
| Federate variance across communities | Single cultural embedding / collapse |

## Industry context (thin ≠ empty)

HEAR/SPEAK remain the funded race (Sahara, NaijaVoices, N-ATLAS, local TTS). Adjacent UNDERSTAND-ish claims exist (e.g. cultural translation / CCI-style frameworks, “nuance” product copy). None of that is proof of exclusivity for an **epistemic gate before action** with provenance-aware correction and anti-coconut metrics — but exclusivity is **not** established until surveyed continuously.

## What would actually lock the niche

Empirical minimum:

1. **False-UNDERSTOOD ≈ 0** on held-out unknown expressions  
2. **False-INSUFFICIENT bounded** (gate not useless)  
3. **≥1 native-speaker-confirmed omission catch** on live input  
4. **Action leakage = 0** under adversarial ambiguity (no tool/side-effect on POSSIBLE_MEANING_LOSS / INSUFFICIENT without approval)  
5. **Correction generalizes out-of-session** without overgeneralization and without treating MODEL_HYPOTHESIS as truth  
6. **≥1 design partner** for whom the **gate** is the purchase reason  
7. Ongoing scan: no adjacent ship of an equivalent gate

Until 1–6 exist, “lock” is aspiration. This document freezes **positioning**, not evidence-gathering.

## Naming (locked once)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **MÍMO** | Product / experience + **epistemic gate** (activation analysis, three states, correction, evidence) |
| **PAL Instant-On** | Voice-to-action application surfaces (Composer, CRM, Field, …) — **execution drafts** under policy |
| **PAL Activation Library** | Seed knowledge store (expressions, candidates, provenance ladder P0–P3) — **not** the product name |
| **Activation (runtime)** | Lookup + representation audit + decision — implemented as `analyze_expression` |

Do not use “PAL Activation” and “MÍMO Activation” as interchangeable product names.

## Benchmark direction (corrected)

Measure: abstention quality, omission detection, ontology traps, **action leakage**, **out-of-session correction generalization** (not only in-session retention).

In-session retention is necessary but trivial. The hard test is generalization without collapse (“all proverbs mean X”).

## Anti-pattern

Treating this niche statement as settled reproduces the coconut failure at strategy layer: a confident frame that cannot see what it is not looking for.

**Freeze the wording. Keep measuring.**
